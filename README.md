## The List of Prerequisites

- **Not ancient**. Both in terms of features and actual performance
- **Ideally sub 5.5", but no larger then 5.8"**
- **Wireless charging & MagSafe**, can be a DIY implementation
- **Customizability**, ideally an ability to load custom ROMs
- **Ideally Type-C and a 3.5 jack**

## The Story Behind the Choice of S10e

My main phone for the past 2 years has been Google's Pixel 6a, not a small phone by any measure at 6.1" and with my relatively small hands and a recent drive to decentralise my phone that is a bit of a problem. Like many other people with similar issues I first looked at Iphone 12 & 13 mini. However with apple's inherent limitations stopped me from pulling the trigger. Now I am willing to live with Lightning port and without a 3.5 jack, for me the main problem lies with the inability to modify the software. I don't necessarily need a custom ROM, but at least an actual launcher. Which as far as I am concerned do not exist on IOS. Now there are some "minimal launchers" on IOS, but those are mostly glorified widgets, wrapped as and app and sold on AppStore. Iphone's also cost money (no shit), around 280-300€ where I live, and have battery problems which would need to be addressed if I buy one.

With all of that said I settled on my old S10e as a viable alternative. First and foremost, I have one on hand. It's also not ancient by any means, both in terms of features and the SOCs. It runs Android 12 at max, which is getting older. However, due to not being an Apple device, the S10e has a whole world of custom ROMs to choose from, some of which are based on the latest Android 16. You can pick your own one [here](https://xdaforums.com/c/samsung-galaxy-s10e.8763/). It is unfortunately a bit larger than I would've wanted to, but at 5.8" it's good enough and certainly smaller than my Pixel. S10e also has 15W wireless charging and can be DIYed into a MagSafe device. It also has a Type-C and a 3.5 jack, which is quite a welcome bonus.
